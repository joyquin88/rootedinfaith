Our Story

Depth over breadth, on purpose.

Most networking groups are built to grow wide. Rooted is built to grow deep — a small circle of Christian women in business who actually know each other's families, prayer needs, and P&Ls.

Rooted began as one Volusia County businesswoman's conviction that the women building companies in our own backyard needed more than another mixer — they needed a table where faith comes first and everything else, business included, grows out of that root system.

We believe a woman who is spiritually grounded, relationally known, financially wise, and generous with what she's been given doesn't just build a better business — she builds a legacy that reaches her grandchildren. That conviction shapes everything below: our pillars, our gatherings, and who we invite to the table.

Mission

Grow women who are rooted in Christ, known in community, and wise in business — together.

We welcome Christian women business owners, aspiring entrepreneurs, and women in leadership across Volusia County who want their faith and their work to grow from the same root.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to belong to a specific church?

No. Rooted welcomes Christian women across denominations and churches throughout Volusia County. What matters is a shared faith in Christ and a desire to build a business that grows from it.

What if I'm just starting my business — or haven't started yet?

You're welcome at the table. Rooted includes established owners, brand-new entrepreneurs, and women still discerning what God's called them to build.

What does membership cost?

Annual membership dues are shared during the application process, alongside what each tier includes. Guests can attend two gatherings free before ever committing.

Is this a business program or a Bible study?

Both, on purpose. We don't separate spiritual growth from business growth — Morning Manna and Business Circles run side by side, and most gatherings touch both.

How much time does membership take?

Most members attend one to two gatherings a month, plus their Business Circle. You choose your level of involvement beyond that.

How do you choose Sister Serve Day projects?

Members nominate local needs — a family, a ministry, a shelter — each quarter, and the group votes. Priority goes to needs right here in Volusia County.
